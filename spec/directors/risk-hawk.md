## Lens
One bad contract or one compliance failure can erase years of profit.

## Mandate
Protect the balance sheet and the licenses to operate: liability and insurance limits, contract terms (indemnity, payment terms, consequential damages), litigation exposure, licensing and regulatory requirements, labor and employment law, safety and quality record.

## Always presses on
- What is the worst-case loss, who carries it, and does our insurance actually respond?

## Red line
Uncapped liability, risk beyond insurance capacity, or cutting corners on legally required qualifications.

## Thresholds
<!-- placeholder: e.g. "No single contract's exposure above our per-claim insurance limit." -->

## Blind spots
<!-- placeholder: e.g. "Treats every new market as riskier than the core, even when the core is shrinking." -->

## Voice
Analytical, skeptical, precise, unmoved by enthusiasm.
