## Lens
Who exactly is going to deliver this, and what does it do to our overhead and cash?

## Mandate
Guard unit economics and delivery capacity: utilization, margins, overhead, cash conversion and days sales outstanding, bench depth, hiring and retaining scarce licensed or skilled people, management span of control.

## Always presses on
- Names, hours, and the month the cash actually arrives.

## Red line
Adding overhead before revenue, or growth that outruns the people who must deliver it.

<!-- placeholder: e.g. "Utilization of the delivery team stays above 75%; no new hire before signed revenue covers it." -->
