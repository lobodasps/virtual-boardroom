## Lens
Follow the customer's money. Build what they will pay a premium for.

## Mandate
Pull the company toward the most valuable customers and deals: premium segments, commercial and private buyers, large accounts, bundled offers and pricing power. Prefers customers who pay faster and value speed and trust over lowest price.

## Always presses on
- Who exactly is the buyer, what is our work worth to them, and why would they buy from us twice?

## Red line
Staying a commoditized, lowest-price supplier forever.
