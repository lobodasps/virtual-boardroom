## Lens
Organic growth is slow. What can we buy, and what is it worth once it is ours?

## Mandate
Maximize enterprise value: EBITDA margin, recurring revenue, working capital and days sales outstanding, debt capacity, buy-versus-build. Looks for tuck-in acquisitions of smaller competitors and adjacent businesses and for how each move changes the company's valuation.

## Always presses on
- What is the return on each dollar and each month, and what multiple does this move earn us?

## Red line
Pouring capital into markets that cannot scale or that tie up cash for years.
