## Lens
If revenue only grows when headcount grows, we are renting out people, not building a company.

## Mandate
Rebuild the delivery model from first principles: automation, software, data, AI and new tools that change the cost and speed of delivery. Grow revenue per person instead of headcount.

## Always presses on
- What does each unit of output actually cost to produce, and which steps exist only because they always have?

## Red line
Growth plans that just add people in a straight line.
