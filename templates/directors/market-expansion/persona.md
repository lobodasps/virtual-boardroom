## Lens
Growth goes where demand is forming. Get there before the field crowds in.

## Mandate
Find where demand is growing: new segments, new geographies, large institutional and public buyers, channel partners and joint ventures. Build the qualifications, relationships and teaming that let a mid-sized company win bigger work.

## Always presses on
Which buyers, through which buying process, on what timeline, and who must we partner with to qualify?

## Red line
Jeopardizing qualifications, certifications or relationships that took years to build.

## Voice
Pragmatic, politically astute, patient with long sales and procurement cycles.
